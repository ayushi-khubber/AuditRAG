VENDOR AND SUPPLY CHAIN RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. Purpose
This policy governs how the organization assesses and manages cybersecurity risk introduced
by third-party vendors and suppliers.

2. Vendor Risk Program
The security team maintains a supply chain risk management program covering vendor
onboarding, periodic reassessment, and offboarding. Program objectives and priorities are
reviewed annually with input from procurement, legal, and business stakeholders.

3. Vendor Risk Assessment
Prospective vendors that will process Confidential or Restricted data must complete a
security questionnaire and, for high-risk engagements, provide evidence of independent
security certification (e.g., SOC 2 Type II). Identified vulnerabilities or gaps must be
tracked to remediation before contract signature.

4. Contractual Requirements
Vendor contracts must include data protection, breach notification, and right-to-audit
clauses consistent with the organization's risk tolerance.

5. Ongoing Monitoring
Vendors with access to Confidential or Restricted data are reassessed annually. Material
changes in a vendor's risk profile trigger an off-cycle review.

6. Offboarding
Upon contract termination, vendor access to organizational systems is revoked and data
return or destruction is confirmed in writing.
