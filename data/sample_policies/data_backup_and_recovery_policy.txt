DATA BACKUP AND RECOVERY POLICY

1. Purpose
This policy defines requirements for backing up organizational data and restoring systems
after a disruptive event.

2. Backup Requirements
All production databases and file shares classified as Internal or higher must be backed up
on a daily incremental and weekly full schedule. Backups must be encrypted at rest and in
transit and stored in a location physically separate from the primary system.

3. Backup Testing
Backup restoration must be tested on a quarterly basis for a sample of critical systems.
Test results, including restoration time, are documented and reviewed by the infrastructure
team lead.

4. Data-at-Rest and Data-in-Transit Protection
Databases containing Confidential or Restricted data must use encryption at rest. Data
transmitted between internal services and to external parties must use TLS 1.2 or higher.

5. Disaster Recovery Execution
In the event that a disaster recovery declaration is made, the infrastructure team executes
the recovery runbook to restore systems in priority order as defined in the business
continuity plan. Recovery progress is communicated to the executive team and affected
business units until services are fully restored.

6. Retention
Backup data is retained according to the data retention schedule and securely deleted once
the retention period expires.
