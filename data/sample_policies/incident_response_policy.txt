INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY

1. Purpose
This policy establishes how the organization detects, analyzes, contains, and communicates
about cybersecurity incidents.

2. Detection and Monitoring
Security-relevant logs from network devices, servers, and endpoints must be forwarded to the
central logging platform. The security operations function monitors these logs and alerts
around the clock for indicators of potentially adverse activity.

3. Incident Declaration and Response
When an event is confirmed as a security incident, the on-call incident commander activates
the incident response plan. Response activities are coordinated with relevant internal teams
and, where applicable, external parties such as forensics vendors or law enforcement.

4. Root Cause Analysis
Following containment, the incident response team performs an analysis to determine the
sequence of events, the root cause, and the scope of impact. Findings are documented in a
post-incident report.

5. Stakeholder Communication
Internal stakeholders are notified according to the severity matrix defined in the incident
response runbook. Legal and compliance teams determine whether external notification to
regulators, customers, or partners is required based on applicable laws and contractual
obligations.

6. Plan Maintenance
The incident response plan is tested annually through a tabletop exercise and updated based
on lessons learned.
