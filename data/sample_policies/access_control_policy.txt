ACCESS CONTROL POLICY

1. Purpose
This policy defines the requirements for managing user identities, authentication, and access
to company systems and data.

2. Identity and Credential Management
All employees, contractors, and service accounts must be issued a unique identity managed
through the corporate identity provider. Credentials must not be shared between users or
systems. Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access and for access to
systems classified as Confidential or higher.

3. Least Privilege and Access Reviews
Access to systems and data must be granted based on job role and reviewed by the resource
owner at least every 90 days. Access permissions must be revoked within 24 hours of an
employee's termination or role change. Elevated (administrative) access requires separate
approval from the employee's manager and the security team, and must be logged.

4. Password and Authentication Standards
Passwords must meet minimum complexity requirements and be rotated in accordance with the
corporate password standard. Service accounts must use vaulted credentials with automatic
rotation where technically feasible.

5. Training
New hires must complete a security awareness training module covering acceptable use,
phishing recognition, and access control responsibilities within their first 30 days of
employment. Refresher training is required annually for all staff.

6. Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
This policy is reviewed annually by the security governance committee and updates are
communicated to all staff.
